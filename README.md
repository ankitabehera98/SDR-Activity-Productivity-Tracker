# SDR Activity & Productivity Tracker

### Sales Operations | SDR Performance Analysis | Google Sheets

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on tracking and analyzing the daily activities and performance of a 10-member Sales Development Representative (SDR) team at a fictional B2B SaaS company.

The objective is to build a structured reporting system to monitor sales activities, measure performance against targets, evaluate individual productivity, and track overall team performance.

The project uses simulated sales data for October 2026 and was developed using Google Sheets.

## 2. Project Objectives

| No. | Objective |
|---|---|
| 1 | Track daily SDR activities, including calls, emails, LinkedIn messages, and follow-ups. |
| 2 | Monitor meetings booked and meetings completed. |
| 3 | Track positive responses and qualified leads. |
| 4 | Compare actual performance against individual targets. |
| 5 | Calculate KPI achievement percentages and conversion rates. |
| 6 | Analyze individual SDR and overall team performance. |
| 7 | Create a KPI dashboard to visualize performance metrics. |
| 8 | Identify performance gaps and provide business recommendations. |

## 4. Workbook Structure

The workbook contains six sheets:

| No. | Sheet Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | SDR Activity Data | Records daily activities for 10 SDRs. |
| 2 | SDR Targets | Maintains individual daily activity targets. |
| 3 | Performance Analysis | Calculates actual performance, target achievement, and conversion rates. |
| 4 | Team Summary | Provides an overall summary of team performance. |
| 5 | KPI Dashboard | Presents key metrics and performance charts. |
| 6 | Project Insights | Highlights performance observations and business recommendations. |

## 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| No. | KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Total Calls Made | Tracks outbound calling activity. |
| 2 | Total Emails Sent | Measures email outreach activity. |
| 3 | Total LinkedIn Messages | Tracks LinkedIn prospecting activity. |
| 4 | Total Follow-ups Completed | Monitors prospect follow-up activity. |
| 5 | Total Meetings Booked | Measures meetings scheduled with prospects. |
| 6 | Total Meetings Completed | Tracks completed meetings. |
| 7 | Total Positive Responses | Measures positive prospect engagement. |
| 8 | Total Leads Qualified | Tracks qualified sales opportunities. |
| 9 | Call Achievement % | Measures calls against the assigned target. |
| 10 | Email Achievement % | Measures emails against the assigned target. |
| 11 | Meeting Completion Rate | Measures completed meetings against booked meetings. |
| 12 | Positive Response Rate | Measures positive responses against total outreach activities. |
| 13 | Lead Qualification Rate | Measures qualified leads against positive responses. |
